# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project should be a place where anyone can participate and learn, regardless of who
they are or how much experience they bring. We listen for impact as much as intent, and
we'd rather fix a problem than argue about whether it counts.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Assume good faith, and stay open to being wrong.
- Give and receive feedback on ideas, not on people.
- When harm happens, prefer repair: acknowledge it, adjust, move on.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, or continuing to engage someone after they've set a boundary.
- Demeaning remarks about a person's character, competence, or belonging.
- Slurs, stereotyping, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery where it isn't welcome.
- Sharing someone's private information without consent.
- Threats, encouragement of harm, or deliberate disruption that makes the space unsafe.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and events.
It also applies when representing the project, such as posting from an official account or
speaking at an event as a project representative.

## Reporting

If something feels off, say so early. Reach the maintainer at
<azelann.borde@gmail.com>, or open a
[draft security advisory](https://github.com/SirEriTheRed/ts-bastion/security/advisories/new)
for private or security-sensitive reports. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly.
Privacy of everyone involved is respected, retaliation isn't tolerated, and only what's
needed to keep people safe gets shared.

## Enforcement

Most issues get resolved with a conversation. When more is needed, steps escalate, and
serious harm can start further down the list.

1. **Notice.** Behavior that clashed with this code. A private note explaining the
   concern, and a different approach going forward.
2. **Limited participation.** A time-limited pause on interaction with the people
   involved, including in spaces outside the project.
3. **Temporary suspension.** A break from project participation with a defined return
   date and expectations shared privately.
4. **Removal.** Permanent removal from project participation. Return only in rare cases,
   after genuine accountability.
